A mana bar for your Claude limits

manabar pins a thin, click-through meter to the bottom of your screen. Session mana drains as you work, the weekly meters sit behind it, and the readout tells you exactly when you refill.

Watch the drain

When tokens burn, a pale ghost lingers where your mana was and fades out, so a hungry agent session is visible from across the room.

Know your refill

The readout shows what's left and when it comes back, for the 5-hour session and both weekly limits.

Every display

One bar per screen, sized to each one, driven by a single poll. Hide them from the tray when you want the desk to yourself.

manabar is in development. A downloadable build for macOS is on the way.
