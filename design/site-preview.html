Claude Code

The session meter up top, both weekly meters in the strip, read from the sign-in your CLI already has.

Codex

Plans with one weekly window get one honest bar; plans with a session window get both, automatically.

Watch the drain

When tokens burn, a pale ghost lingers where your session was and fades out, so a hungry agent session is visible from across the room.

Know your refill

The readout shows what's left and when it comes back, for the 5-hour session and both weekly limits.

Every display

One bar per screen, sized to each one, driven by a single poll. Hide them from the tray when you want the desk to yourself.
