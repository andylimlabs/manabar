manabar · UI proposals

Five directions grounded in MMO and gacha meter research. Mocks are at true height, pinned to the bottom edge of a fake desktop.

Research digest

  • Draw the future on the bar. WoW rested XP renders a pale ghost segment ahead of the fill showing bonus runway. The future is spatial, not textual.
  • Ticks create vocabulary. WoW's 20 bubbles let players say "half a bubble left". Segments make a long-lived bar quantifiable at a glance.
  • Two-layer change display. Fighting games snap the true value instantly, then a ghost layer lingers (SF6: 2s hold) so you can see what you just spent.
  • Integer current/max, never percent. Genshin resin (45/200), HSR power, Arknights sanity. Numbers in spendable units read as "runs remaining".
  • Absolute clock beats countdown for planning. "Fully replenished at 7:40 AM" is what players act on. Countdown only for the short loop.
  • Urgency ladder. Bar, then flashing bar, then literal digits. The closer to empty, the more explicit the readout (Sonic drowning, BotW stamina).
  • State is a whole-fill recolor (WoW purple to blue), not a badge. Icons only for "currently accruing" (FFXIV moon).
  • Conditional visibility. BotW's stamina wheel appears only while draining. A meter that only matters sometimes should only exist sometimes.

Sourced from WoW FrameXML, FFXIV job gauges + Simple Mode, SF6 frame data, Genshin/HSR/FGO/Arknights wikis, Game UI Database, F2P energy-system literature.

P1

Bubbles · the WoW exp bar, done properly

Refine the current strip with the three details that make WoW's bar iconic: a spark riding the fill edge, ticks promoted to real bubble segments, and the pill speaking in bubbles and clock time instead of raw percent.

normal · 84% session left, spark at fill edge
17 bubbles · resets 8:40 PM · week 79%

Keeps

  • Current architecture, pure polish
  • Bubble vocabulary: "2 bubbles left tonight"
  • Spark makes drain visible in peripheral vision

Costs

  • Least ambitious of the five
  • Percent still the underlying unit
P2

Ghost drain · the fighting game two-layer bar

When tokens burn, the fill snaps down and a pale ghost segment lingers where the mana used to be, decaying over about a minute. A heavy agent session leaves a visible bite mark. Burn rate becomes spatial: fat ghost means fast burn.

mid-burn · 62% left, ghost shows the 8% consumed by the last few minutes
62% · -8% / 10 min · at this pace tapped by 6:10 PM · resets 8:40 PM

Keeps

  • Answers "what is eating my bar" at a glance
  • Pairs naturally with a pace projection readout
  • Composable with P1 or P5

Costs

  • Needs burn-rate state kept client-side between polls
  • 60s poll granularity makes the ghost chunky, not smooth
P3

Resin pill · the gacha readout, no bar at all

Genshin grammar: a gem icon plus integer current/max in a floating pill, with the absolute refill clock underneath. Session mapped to a 200 point mana pool. Optionally invisible above half, appearing only when the pool matters (BotW rule).

corner pill only · 168/200 mana, full-width strip gone
168/200
resets 8:40 PM · week 158/200 · fable 158/200

Keeps

  • Smallest footprint, zero full-width presence
  • Integer pool reads as owned currency, not a quota
  • Conditional visibility keeps the desktop clean

Costs

  • The 200 pool is invented: API only gives percent
  • Loses the ambient exp-bar fantasy that started this
  • No peripheral-vision signal while working
P4

Timer stack · the raid boss-mod frame

DBM/BigWigs grammar: every limit is its own mini bar with the name on the left and time on the right, stacked in the corner and sorted by which binds first. Session, week, and fable get equal, uniform treatment. Scales automatically if Anthropic adds meters.

  • Corner block competes with app windows more than an edge strip
P5 · REC

Hybrid HUD · exp bar body, gacha brain, ghost layer

Keep the full-width exp strip as the ambient layer (P1 polish: spark, bubbles). Give the pill the gacha grammar: absolute reset clock, at-pace projection, and the urgency ladder. Fold in the P2 ghost for recent burn. States below show the ladder.

state 1 · healthy: calm strip, clock-time pill
84% · resets 8:40 PM · fable 79%
state 2 · low: amber recolor of the whole fill, projection surfaces
24% · tapped by ~6:10 PM at this pace · resets 8:40 PM
state 3 · tapped: red, digits take over, countdown becomes the content
TAPPED · mana back at 8:40 PM (1h 12m)

Keeps

  • The ambient MMO fantasy plus the gacha planning readout
  • Whole-fill recolor makes state legible from peripheral vision
  • Every element earned its place in some game's HUD

Costs

  • Most work: ghost state, projection math, ladder logic
  • Projection needs a burn-rate source (client history or ccusage)

Why recommended: P1 alone is cosmetic, P3 abandons the ambient bar, P4 is a raid frame not a desktop companion. P5 is the exp bar you asked for with the two research findings that matter most folded in: the absolute reset clock (planning) and the ghost layer (what just happened). P2 and P1 are strict subsets of it, so we can ship it in stages: polish, then clock grammar, then ghost.

P5 · DEEP DIVE

Anatomy, pacing segments, and the P4 toggle

Every element labeled. The bar is three independent layers over one data model: the strip (ambient state), the ghost (recent change), the pill (exact numbers). Each can ship separately.

annotated · mid-session, 62% left, burning slightly hot
1 fill · mana left (62%)
4 pace notch · even-burn line (45%)
2 ghost · last ~10 min of burn
3 spark + 5 weekly hairline
6 62% · resets 8:40 PM · fable 79%

1 Fill: budget remaining, drains right to left, whole thing recolors by state (indigo, amber, red). 2 Ghost: when a poll shows a drop, the lost width lingers pale and decays over ~60s. A fat ghost means something is eating tokens right now. 3 Spark: marks the live edge; its pulse is the only always-on animation. 4 Pace notch: the WoW rested-tick idea inverted. It sits where your mana would be if you burned perfectly evenly across the 5h window (100% at reset start, 0% at reset). Fill right of the notch: you are ahead of schedule, relax. Fill left: running hot. This answers "should I worry" with zero text and zero math. 5 Weekly hairline: most-binding weekly meter, ambient only. 6 Pill: exact numbers, absolute clock, and the projection when a state demands it.

segments · 20 bubbles (WoW default, 5% each, no meaning)
segments · 10 bubbles = 30 min each at even pace (session rec)
segments · weekly promoted to its own 5px bar with 14 bubbles = one per half-day (Andy's idea, toggle/alt view)

Why tie count to the clock: segments quantized by the reset window turn the bar into a schedule. Session = 5h, so 10 bubbles means "spend a bubble every half hour and you exactly make it". Weekly = 7 days, so 14 bubbles means "a bubble per half-day". 20 would also work visually but the bubbles would mean nothing. On the 2px ambient hairline ticks are invisible, so the 14 bubbles only appear in the promoted view below.

Where P4 fits (your toggle instinct): P4 becomes the detail view of the same data, not a rival. Two triggers: manual (tray menu "Detail view", swaps the corner pill for the timer stack) and automatic (the API marks which limit is currently binding via is_active; when a weekly overtakes the session as your real constraint, its bar auto-promotes into view for as long as that holds, BotW conditional-visibility rule). Ambient mode stays one strip + one hairline + one pill.

Behavior over one evening (the state machine): 7:00 PM, fresh window: full indigo bar, pill says "100% · resets 12:40 AM". You work; the spark walks left, ghosts flare and fade behind it as agents run. 9:30 PM: fill crosses the pace notch going the wrong way; nothing changes color yet, but you can see you are left of the line. 10:15 PM, 28% left: whole fill recolors amber, pill grows the projection "tapped by ~11:20 PM at this pace". 11:20 PM, 8%: red. Tapped: bar turns to a dim red wash, pill becomes a countdown "mana back at 12:40 AM (1h 04m)". 12:40 AM: bar refills full in one sweep with a brief golden flash (the level-up moment), back to calm indigo. The weekly hairline barely moved all night; on Sunday it is the thing that matters, and that is when it auto-promotes.
